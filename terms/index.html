By reserving a spot you agree to the terms below. Please read them before paying — they are short and written in plain language on purpose.

1. What the Academy is

The Portrait Realism Academy is a 3-day, in-person black & grey portrait realism masterclass taught by Federico Galliani.

  • Dates: 6, 7 and 8 October 2026
  • Location: SOL Studios, 11000 W Janesville Rd, Hales Corners (Milwaukee), WI 53130, USA
  • Capacity: 10 artists — no exceptions
  • Language: English
  • Who it is for: working tattoo artists at intermediate or advanced level

Included: a full workstation, all working materials, premium ink and grey-wash sets, synthetic practice skin, catering on all three days, and a certificate of completion. You bring only your own tattoo machine.

On Day 2 you observe Federico tattooing a live model provided by the host studio. On Day 3 you practise on synthetic skin under direct supervision — participants do not tattoo human clients during the Academy.

Your spot is confirmed only when the deposit has been received and we have sent you a written confirmation.

4. Refunds and cancellations

If your application is not accepted — you receive a full refund, immediately. No conditions.

If we cancel the Academy — you receive a full refund of everything you have paid. We are not liable for travel or accommodation you booked separately, so please consider travel insurance.

If you change your mind — the deposit is non-refundable, because your spot is one of only ten and is taken off the market for you. However, it is transferable: you can pass your spot to another artist, or move it to the next edition of the Academy. Just write to us.

If you have already paid the balance and cancel more than 30 days before the start date, the balance (USD 1,500) is refunded and only the deposit is retained. Within 30 days of the start date, the balance can be transferred to another artist or to a future edition, but is not refunded in cash.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and normally appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Refunds are processed by writing to academy@portraitrealismacademy.com.
